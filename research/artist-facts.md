# Kaysin: research and design rationale

Research date: October 18, 2026. This is a source-linked public-profile study, not a claim to know everything about the person. No private accounts were accessed. No Wikipedia sources were used.

## The useful distinction

Kaysin has three connected roles: DJ, producer, and label A&R. The last is what separates his story from an interchangeable DJ homepage. He makes records and also helps choose the records that other people hear. The reimagination makes all three roles visible instead of treating the site as a large photo followed by generic embeds.

## Biography and background

- Resident Advisor identifies Kaysin as a Los Angeles producer, DJ and label A&R. It says he started producing and DJing while attending college in Santa Barbara. The biography explicitly looks ahead to 2021, so it is historical background, not current-year activity. https://ra.co/dj/kaysin/biography
- His current official site, Beatport profile and SoundCloud description list A&R duties across Repopulate Mars, South of Saturn and North of Neptune. https://www.kaysin.com/home ; https://www.beatport.com/artist/kaysin/641789 ; https://soundcloud.com/kaysin
- In his first-person interview with Wenzday, Kaysin explains that he met Lee Foss when both were playing Groove Cruise, helped with the new label, and eventually became its full-time A&R. He says being a producer helps him understand submitted music and artists' needs. The interview accompanies Your Fantasy, from 2024; its relative dates and future plans must not be presented as 2026 news. https://www.musicis4lovers.com/interview-we-sat-down-with-wenzday-kaysin-to-chat-about-their-latest-release-on-repopulate-mars/
- The same interview emphasizes friendship before collaboration, the original Haylee Wood vocal, months of iterations on Your Fantasy, and Lee Foss' direct feedback. Useful tone: involved, collaborative, grounded in records and people, not a fictional futurist DJ persona.
- Beatportal's label profile discusses Kaysin within the Repopulate Mars/South of Saturn family. The article's narrative refers to 2021 and is historical context, irrespective of migrated image timestamps. https://www.beatportal.com/articles/39183-label-of-the-month-repopulate-mars

## What the original site does and what changes

Observed in a real browser: official white wordmark; navigation for Home/Music/Tour/About; NEW MUSIC hero over a live photo; Bandsintown script; Spotify playlist and YouTube embed; long bio; management/booking contact; newsletter UI. Original image names are not reliable subject labels: iktg-25.JPG is a crowd/club photo, while a file called Screenshot 2022-10-13 191646.png contains the actual portrait.

Reimagined direction: a record sleeve, not a festival ticket portal. Orange #ff6738, ink #191b18, warm paper #f2f0e7. The existing mark is retained and traced to SVG for crisp large-scale use. Real press portrait overlaps it. Barlow Condensed supplies the tall release/editorial headlines; DM Sans handles labels and body. Both are locally hosted with OFL licenses.

The site has a six-record selector with authentic Apple preview audio, release artwork and outbound links; click-to-load Day Trip video; accurate artist-origin/A&R story; public contact directory; real playlist and show-follow paths. No dead newsletter form, fake ticket inventory, invented popularity metric, automatic audio or generic reveal animations.

## Additional primary-source cross-check

ADE explicitly identifies Kaysin as Nick Sommers, an LA-based DJ/producer, and confirms his A&R work for Repopulate Mars and South of Saturn. This is a published professional identity, not an inference from the interview's first-name references.[1]

Sweetest Lines has different platform dates: the Apple item used for the preview is dated 2024-07-12, while Beatport's RPM217 release page gives 2024-06-28. Preserve both attributions rather than claiming a single universal original-release date. The visible site shows the uncontested year, 2024, so its copy requires no change.[2]

## Evidence limits and launch requirements

This is an independent redesign preview, not an authorized edit or deployment of kaysin.com. Publicly accessible artwork and press photos remain their owners' property; confirm usage rights before a real launch. The supplied biography's chart/support claims were not independently audited as numeric outcomes and are not turned into UI badges. No private identity, age, hometown or legal name is inferred from interview references to Nick. Tour inventory is a point-in-time observation. External platforms can change and may require consent or a login in the visitor's region.
